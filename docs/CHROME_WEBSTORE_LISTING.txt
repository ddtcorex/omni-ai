Omni AI — Your All-in-One AI Writing & Browsing Assistant

Stop switching tabs to use ChatGPT, Gemini, or Claude. Omni AI brings the world's leading AI models directly into your browser — write better emails on Gmail, polish code comments on GitHub, or summarize a research paper, all without leaving the page.

Highlight any text and get instant AI help exactly where you need it: standard inputs, textareas, and rich-text editors like TinyMCE and other contenteditable-based editors.

---

WHAT MAKES OMNI AI DIFFERENT

Most AI extensions lock you into a subscription and a single model. Omni AI doesn't.

• Bring Your Own Key (BYOK) — Use your own API key for Gemini, OpenAI, or Groq. Pay providers directly for what you actually use, nothing more.
• Custom Gateway Support — Connect any OpenAI-compatible endpoint: DeepSeek, OpenRouter, a self-hosted proxy, or your own gateway.
• Universal Editor Support — Works inside plain inputs, textareas, and contenteditable rich-text editors (TinyMCE and similar) across the web.
• Clean, Focused UI — A glassmorphic floating button appears right where you're working and gets out of the way when you don't need it (can be turned off entirely in Settings, keyboard shortcuts still work).

FEATURES

Selection Menu (the floating star icon)
Highlight text anywhere on the page to trigger it, positioned right at your cursor:
• Grammar & Spelling Fix
• Rephrase — fresh, natural wording every time
• Summarize — turn a 10-minute read into a 30-second one
• Change Tone — Professional, Casual, Friendly, Direct, or Confident presets
• Explain — break down jargon, legal text, or technical concepts

Right-Click Menu
Select text, right-click, and pick: Translate, Rephrase, Add Emoji, Summarize, or Ask Omni AI — no need to open the floating button first.

Write In Place
Get an AI-improved version of your draft and click Replace to update it directly inside the editor — Gmail, LinkedIn, a CMS, wherever you're typing.

Chat & Quick Ask
• Quick Ask (Alt+A) — a floating overlay to ask about the text you've highlighted without losing your place.
• Popup Chat — a side panel (click the toolbar icon) that keeps your recent conversation for longer research or brainstorming sessions.

Translation
10 languages supported: English, Vietnamese, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Japanese, Korean, and Chinese. Set your primary language once — Smart Translate then auto-detects the source and translates in whichever direction makes sense.

QUICK SETUP

Omni AI needs one API key to work. Pick a provider:

Google Gemini (free tier available)
1. Get a key at aistudio.google.com/app/apikey
2. Omni AI Settings → AI Provider → Google Gemini → paste key → Save

OpenAI
1. Get a key at platform.openai.com/api-keys
2. Select OpenAI in Settings for GPT-4o / GPT-4o mini

Groq (fast inference, free tier available)
1. Get a key at console.groq.com/keys
2. Select Groq in Settings for Llama 3.3, DeepSeek-R1, and more

Custom Gateway (any OpenAI-compatible API)
1. Get a key from your provider (DeepSeek, OpenRouter, a self-hosted gateway, etc.)
2. Enter the base URL, your key, and the model name in Settings

KEYBOARD SHORTCUTS

• Alt+A — Quick Ask about the current selection
• Alt+R — Rephrase selected text
• Alt+T — Smart Translate selected text
• Alt+F — Fix Grammar on selected text

Summarize and Explain are also available as commands — assign your own shortcut for them (and customize any of the above) at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.
