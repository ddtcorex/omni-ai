Omni AI: Your All-in-One AI Writing & Browsing Assistant

Omni AI puts the world's leading AI models directly inside your browser, so you never have to switch tabs again. Highlight any text on any page, whether it is an email you are drafting, a GitHub comment, a research paper, or a foreign article, and get instant AI help right where you are working.

WHY INSTALL IT
The AI you already pay for, working on the pages you already use. No new subscription, no copying text back and forth between browser and ChatGPT/Gemini/Claude, and no lock-in to a single provider. Omni AI bolts AI onto your normal workflow instead of pulling you into another tab.

WHAT IT DOES
• Fix Grammar & Spelling: polish anything in one click
• Rephrase: get fresh, natural wording every time
• Summarize: turn a 10-minute read into a 30-second one
• Change Tone: Professional, Casual, Friendly, Direct, or Confident presets
• Explain: break down jargon, legal text, or technical concepts
• Translate: 43 languages, from Spanish, French and German to Hindi, Arabic, Thai, Vietnamese and both Simplified and Traditional Chinese. Set a primary language for one-click translation, or use Smart Translate, which auto-detects the source language for you.
• Add Emoji: liven up a message in one click

Works in plain inputs, textareas, and rich-text editors such as TinyMCE. Trigger it from the floating button, the right-click menu, or keyboard shortcuts. A side panel adds one-click Summarize, Smart Translate, and Explain for the whole page you are reading.

THE INTERFACE SPEAKS YOUR LANGUAGE
The entire extension interface ships in 52 locales, covering 46 languages. Six of those locales are regional variants: British English, Canadian French, Latin American Spanish, European Portuguese, and Traditional Chinese for Hong Kong and Taiwan. All 52:

WHAT MAKES OMNI AI DIFFERENT
Most AI extensions lock you into a subscription and a single model. Omni AI does not.
• Bring Your Own Key (BYOK): use your own API key for Google Gemini, OpenAI, Groq, or Anthropic Claude. Pay the provider directly for what you actually use, nothing more.
• Custom Gateway Support: connect any OpenAI-compatible endpoint, such as DeepSeek, OpenRouter, a self-hosted proxy, or your own gateway.
• Universal Editor Support: plain inputs, textareas, and contenteditable rich-text editors across the web.
• Clean, Focused UI: a glassmorphic floating button appears where you are working and gets out of the way when you do not need it (it can be turned off entirely in Settings).

QUICK SETUP
Omni AI needs one API key to work. Pick a provider:
• Google Gemini: aistudio.google.com/app/apikey (free tier available)
• OpenAI: platform.openai.com/api-keys
• Groq: console.groq.com/keys (fast inference, free tier available)
• Anthropic Claude: console.anthropic.com/settings/keys
• Custom Gateway: enter your base URL, key, and model in Settings

KEYBOARD SHORTCUTS
• Alt+O: Show the Quick Actions menu at the current selection
• Alt+R: Rephrase selected text
• Alt+T: Smart Translate selected text
• Alt+F: Fix Grammar on selected text
Quick Ask, Summarize, and Explain are also available as commands. Assign your own shortcut (and customize any of the above) at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.

USAGE DASHBOARD
Settings → Usage shows total actions run, words processed, and words generated, a quick look at how much time Omni AI is saving you.

PRIVACY
• No middlemen: your browser talks directly to the AI provider's API. We do not operate a backend that sees your prompts.
• No tracking: no browsing history, prompt logging, or identity collection.
• Local storage only: your API keys stay in your local Chrome profile.

SUMMARY (132 character limit)
The manifest ships a localized name and summary rather than hardcoded
strings: `manifest.json` declares `"name": "__MSG_extName__"` and
`"description": "__MSG_extDescription__"`, and Chrome resolves both from
the active `_locales/<locale>/messages.json`. That is what Chrome itself
displays in all 52 shipped locales, on chrome://extensions, in the
extension menu and in the install prompt.

The Chrome Web Store listing page is a separate surface: its title and
summary come from the dashboard fields, not from the manifest, so those
are English today. Localizing the store page itself is optional and is
done in the dashboard's language tab.

English source strings (`_locales/en/messages.json`):

DATA USAGE DISCLOSURES
Answer the dashboard's data collection questions exactly as written in the
declaration block of `docs/PERMISSION_JUSTIFICATIONS.txt`: the extension
sends the selected text and, for Page Tools, the active page's text
directly to the AI provider the user configured, using the user's own API
key. It does not send that data to any Omni AI backend, does not store it,
does not use it for advertising, and does not sell or share it.
