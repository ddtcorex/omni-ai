| 1   | T11 review | **SSE-split corruption**: `buffer.split(/(?=data:/)` also splits _inside JSON string values_. Any delta payload containing a literal `data:` cuts mid-token; both fragments fail `JSON.parse` and vanish through the silent `catch {}` blocks, so streamed text is silently truncated. Minimal repro (verified): for the event `data: {"choices":[{"delta":{"content":"see data: below"}}]}\n\n`, the split yields `"data: {\"choices\":[{\"delta\":{\"content\":\"see"` and `"data: below\"}}]}"` — neither parses, and the whole delta is lost. Fix by splitting on event boundaries (`\n\n`) or parsing with a streaming-aware buffer instead of a lookahead regex. | `lib/providers/custom-gateway.js:87` (drops at `:104`, `:114`, `:131`, `:140`) | Next touch of the streaming parser                         |
